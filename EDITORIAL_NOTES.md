Chapters 1–3 are carried over word for word from the approved revision. The only front-matter changes: the series line now reads "Quiet Hollow Mystery — Book One", and the "Revised manuscript — Chapters 1–3" line is removed. Chapters 4–18 are revised and expanded. ## 1. Major continuity problems corrected

**The jacket chain**
- The original Ch. 14 receipt was one jacket, size medium, dated September 15. It is now a Kessler's special-order ticket dated 6/14: two HarborLine women's chore coats, navy, size small, paid cash, "HOLD FOR: N. PETRAKIS."
- Nora gave Rowan her jacket at the **Fourth of July** bonfire, not in September. Rowan's last post (August) shows her wearing it, and the caption keeps "favorite hand-me-down."
- **Rowan's jacket vanished with her and is never recovered.** Camille died in Nora's own matching jacket.
- Wren works out that there were two jackets herself, before the receipt. In Ch. 12–13 she finds an instant photo of Rowan in the new jacket, with a second plaid cuff at the edge of the frame.
- Darryl's "couple months back" is now **April**, which ties to Camille's 17th birthday (April 9).

**The shoes and the scene**
- The original confession had Nora taking the jacket off Camille's body. That is gone. Camille is still wearing the jacket, and Nora returns before dawn for the **right shoe**.
- **How the right shoe came off:** it came off in Nora's hand when she grabbed for Camille.
- **Why Nora went back for it:** her prints were on it, and it lay on the ledge at the railing, which showed someone had been standing with Camille.
- **The cut:** she cut her **right palm** on the rusted rail reaching through to get it.
- **The left shoe:** Camille never untied her shoes. She stepped out of the left one when she pulled away from Nora on the grass. Priya plants this in Ch. 9.
- **The right shoe is found** in Nora's closet during the search (Ch. 16), which pays off "I still had a shoe to find."

**The timeline**
- Rowan's last post was "three years" before in the original Ch. 4; it is now six.
- The 11:52 freeze is explained: Camille paused her location because Nora asked her to.
- The Fuel Stop footage now shows Nora's car going out at 11:26 PM and back at 12:16 AM, then out again at 4:58 AM and back at 5:23 AM. One headlight is dim.
- The DVR overwrites after seven days, which gives the Fuel Stop visit a deadline.
- Nora texts Theo at 5:47 AM, before Gary's 911 call at 6:24.

**Theo's alibi**
- This is now concrete. Colby drove Theo and waited (his brake lights are in frame).
- Colby re-captioned two real photos as "11:15" and "11:52". The shared album shows they were taken at 12:21 and 12:38.
- Kenzie confirms Theo was gone about 40 minutes, and so was Colby's car.

**How Nora knew Wren was asking about the jacket**
- Ch. 7: Priya says it out loud in her aunt's kitchen.
- Ch. 9: Theo also hears it from Priya by phone.
- The warning text came from a throwaway texting-app number. The "T" phone was a separate prepaid phone.

**The break-in**
- The original had no explanation, and Cal was home during it. It now happens during Camille's funeral reception. Nora calls Cal beforehand to make sure he and Wren will attend.
- She gets in with the garage-door trick, planted in Ch. 5 ("whoever was watching us on Saturdays"). Cal reveals that Rowan called the trick "the Nora."

**How the case is cracked**
- The original confession happened at Nora's house after an informal visit by Reyes, Wren and Priya. Now the evidence builds in steps:
  1. Theo tells Wren and Priya the truth.
  2. Theo gives Reyes a statement. A minor's report of sexual abuse goes to the county DA, so Boone cannot bury it.
  3. Warrants are issued.
  4. The search finds the cedar blocks, the right shoe and Rowan's thank-you note.
  5. Nora is found at Hollow Point and confesses.
- Physical evidence corroborates the confession:
  - the blood swab from the railing
  - prints on the shoe
  - the phone records
  - the burner top-ups bought with her rewards card

**Charges**
- Nora is now charged with sexual abuse of a minor, tampering with physical evidence, tampering with a witness, burglary, hindering and obstruction. The DA is still reviewing the death itself. The wording is kept general on purpose (no statute numbers).

**Framing of the relationship**
- Theo was **16** when it started and Nora was 47. The narration and Wren's dialogue say outright that this was not an "affair."
- Theo is still responsible for lying and for the false alibi. The DA declines to charge him given his cooperation and circumstances.

**Smaller fixes**
- "Marisol Ochoa" is now Kenzie.
- "Cole Bricker's party" is now Colby Reed's house. Colby runs the server.
- Sasha's feud is dated September, not October. She is cleared by a stream archive.
- Des and Wren have been friends for eight years, not ten.
- Reyes's tenure stays consistent: 14 months in Ch. 8, 15 months in Ch. 18.
- Camille's archive visit is now dated (9/23) on Ms. Feiler's sign-in sheet. She photographed the poster and asked whether they ever found the jacket.

## 3. Blurb items to update

- **"Wren has ninety-six hours before the case goes cold"** is not supported. The investigation runs about four and a half weeks (mid-October to mid-November). The only real deadline is the Fuel Stop camera's seven-day overwrite. Suggest dropping the countdown, or using something true, such as "before the only camera on the road erases the night."
- **"Even a cheating boyfriend or two"** contradicts approved Ch. 2, where Wren says she doesn't take cheating cases. The same claim appears in the Pitch Package one-paragraph pitch and the query letter; update all three.
- **"(formerly titled 'What the Tide Kept')"** should be removed before the copy is used anywhere public.
- **"This time, she might find out what really happened to her sister"** promises more than Book One delivers. Rowan's case deliberately stays open. Suggest "...she might finally be ready to find out what happened to her sister."
- **Optional:** "With her best friend Des running the tech side" is accurate but undersells Des. Priya's alliance is described correctly.
- **Still accurate:** the missing shoe, the jacket Wren "swears she's seen in an old photo of Rowan" (still true; the two-jacket twist sits underneath it), and "the police call it an accident."
